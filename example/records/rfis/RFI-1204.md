# RFI-1204: Canopy edge beam conflict with roof drain leader at Grid C-7

## Question

Drawing A-501 shows the canopy edge beam continuous through Grid C-7 while
P-210 routes the roof drain leader through the same web location. The GC asked
whether to reroute the leader or modify the beam, requesting a response by
2025-06-02 to hold the steel fabrication start ([letter, lines 14-24](../../Originals/rfis/RFI-1204-2025-05-12.txt)).

## Responses

The architect of record directed the leader rerouted east of Grid C-7 per
sketch SK-A-044 with a field-welded reinforcing angle at the revised
penetration, structural engineer concurring, and flagged anticipated cost
impact ([letter, lines 26-31](../../Originals/rfis/RFI-1204-2025-05-12.txt)).

## Resolution

Closed 2025-06-05 with cost impact Yes. The GC opened CE 455 to track pricing;
the pricing arrived as [PCO-217](../pcos/PCO-217.md)
([letter, lines 33-36](../../Originals/rfis/RFI-1204-2025-05-12.txt)).
